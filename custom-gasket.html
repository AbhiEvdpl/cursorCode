Advanced Technology

CNC Gasket Cutting

Our advanced flash cutter fabricates custom gaskets with impressive speed and accuracy, making it ideal for a wide variety of applications. No individual tooling is required to cut soft and semi-rigid materials, which significantly simplifies the manufacturing process.

Our knife cutting systems guarantee quality, reliability, and high performance. Flash cutting is recognized as the fastest and most cost-effective process for producing flat gaskets — perfect for businesses of all sizes.

The flash gasket cutter utilizes precise CNC control to accurately cut materials ranging from .001" to .750" in thickness. Material is carefully pulled with negative air pressure to lie perfectly flat, ensuring a true and consistent cut every time.

Additionally, our overhead projector system enables perfect nesting of gasket products, maximizing material efficiency and delivering lower-cost products to our customers. We also offer versatile waterjet cutting services for even more options.
