CNC Gasket Cutting

Our advanced flash cutter can fabricate custom gaskets with impressive speed and accuracy, making it an ideal solution for a variety of applications. Remarkably, no individual tooling is necessary to effectively cut soft and semi-rigid materials, which simplifies the manufacturing process significantly.

Our knife cutting systems guarantee quality, reliability, and high performance, ensuring that each gasket meets the necessary specifications. Flash cutting is recognized as the fastest and least expensive process for producing flat gaskets, which is beneficial for many businesses.

The flash gasket cutter utilizes precise CNC control to accurately cut materials ranging from .001" to .750" in thickness. The material is carefully pulled with negative air pressure to lie flat on the table, which ensures a true and consistent cut.

This capability allows for custom options and faster production turnaround times, catering to the dynamic needs of our clients. CNC gasket cutting enables our clients to access quick prototypes, accommodate large production runs, and manage all order sizes in between, enhancing flexibility in their operations.

Additionally, the flash cutter employs an overhead projector, granting our gasket maker the ability to perfectly nest the gasket products, which maximizes material efficiency and ultimately contributes to a lower-cost product. We also offer a versatile water cutting service for gasket parts, providing even more options for our customers.
