Vertical Machining

A machining center is a CNC machine tool that can perform different operations like milling, boring, and drilling, with high accuracy and minimal time. It consists of an automatic tool-changing mechanism that enables it to use multiple cutting tools during the machining process.

Laser Engraving

Laser engraving is the most popular part marking technology in product identification, traceability, & repeatability. Laser engraving technology is highly accurate — using a laser to make permanent markings on different materials. It is the optimal choice for marking parts & products in many industries, especially aerospace & aeronautics.

Lathe Machining

A lathe is a machine tool used to shape metal. It furnishes a metal piece by rotating on an axis while stationary cutting tooling removes unwanted material from the workpiece — ultimately forming a finished shape. Used for various product fabricating operations such as sanding, drilling, cutting, deformation, and turning, they are employed in the metalworking, thermal spraying, glass processing, and wood turning of various artistic products.

Shearing

Shearing machines are versatile, multipurpose devices widely employed in the precise cutting of various types of alloys and other sheet metal materials. Some shearing machines utilize a scissor-like, angular shear action to effectively cut metal into sheets or strips of desired dimensions with accuracy and efficiency.
