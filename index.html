Financial Planning & Analysis

FP&A built for execution

We build and operate models, cashflow forecasts, and unit economics that are meant to drive decisions. Our FP&A work directly informs real tradeoffs around inventory, marketing spend, hiring, and capital strategy.

We can work within your existing internal models, leverage third-party forecasting systems, or build a bespoke model. The differentiator isn't the model itself – it's our ability to analyze, synthesize, and operate off the data in real time to guide strategic and tactical financial decisions.

The outcome is FP&A that actually gets used. The goal isn't perfect projections. It's better decisions, faster.

Our FP&A models have been battle-tested and is the foundation logic driving billions of dollars of revenue of CPG brands.

Accounting

Accounting built for CPG operators

Our accounting is structured to support execution, not just compliance. We focus on accurate categorization, inventory-aware COGS, and channel-level clarity so you understand what's actually driving performance such as your customer acquisition cost per channel.
