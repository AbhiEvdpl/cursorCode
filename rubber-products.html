Material Guide

Sheet Rubber Specifications

NEOPRENE RUBBER

A general purpose compound with low temperature flexibility and oil resistance. Resists rotting, checking, and cracking due to weather exposure. Commonly used for gaskets, sealing, skirting, cushioning, or stripping rubber material. Great for rubber washers and bushings.

SLAB NEOPRENE RUBBER

High-grade commercial neoprene offered in heavy gauges. These high-quality slabs lay flat, rather than bent or rolled, to make cutting easier. Used for machinery mounting pads, wear strips, shock pads and where medium oil and ozone resistance is required.

RED RUBBER

An economical red SBR (Styrene Butadiene) sheet for use in low pressure applications with no oil resistant requirements. Red rubber remains the industry's general service gasket material for common applications such as water.

GUM RUBBER

Excellent physical properties such as superior resilience, tensile, elongation, and abrasion resistance. Tan floating stock is produced entirely with FDA approved ingredients. Non-marking and safe to use for repeated food exposure.

EPDM RUBBER

For use in applications requiring higher temperature ranges, ozone, chemical and weather resistance.

CLOTH INSERTED RUBBER

Where applications call for maximum conformity and deformation resistance. Fabric reinforced rubber presents good dimensional stability under high compression loads. Custom compounding available.

NITRILE RUBBER

For use in applications requiring a rubber sheet or gasket with high resistance to petroleum based fluids.

WHITE FDA NITRILE RUBBER

A sheet specially formulated for its whiteness, used in food, pharmaceutical, and cosmetic processing. Made from FDA approved ingredients per 21CFR 177.2600, it is non-marking, non-allergenic, and safe for repeated food exposure.

VITON® RUBBER

A premium grade fluoro-elastomer sheet for the most demanding applications. Excellent heat and chemical resistance, but not suitable for flex fuels containing high levels of alcohol or MTBE.

SILICONE RUBBER

Designed for applications where extreme high and low temperature resistance is needed. Provides excellent UV and ozone resistance, is non-toxic, chemically inert and fungus resistant.

HYPALON® RUBBER

With resistance to most chemicals, temperature extremes, ultraviolet light, and oil, Hypalon® chlorosulfonated polyethylene is used in industrial and automotive applications where high performance is critical.

BUTYL RUBBER

Exceptionally low gas and moisture permeability with outstanding resistance to heat aging, weather, ozone, chemical attack, flexing, abrasion and tearing. Excellent electrical insulation performance.

SPONGE RUBBER

Available plain or with PSA backing. Excellent compression seal against air, moisture, light and dust penetration. Blend of Neoprene and EPDM makes this material moderately resistant to oil and ozone.

CORK RUBBER

A mixture of first grade granulated cork and synthetic rubber polymer. Combines the high resilience of rubber with the compressibility of cork. Manufactured under heat and pressure.

AASHTO BEARING PAD

Neoprene Bearing Pads provide uniform load transfer from beam to substructure. Permit beam rotation, absorb vibration, prevent sound transfer. Used extensively in bridge structures and precast concrete buildings.

SKIRTBOARD RUBBER

Abrasion resistant, flexible, and excellent for use as conveyor skirting, chute lining, snow plow blades, and more.
