Sheet Rubber Specifications

All sheet goods manufactured to the highest quality standards at competitive prices

NEO

Neoprene Rubber

General purpose compound with low temperature flexibility and oil resistance. Resists rotting, checking, and cracking due to weather exposure. Commonly used for gaskets, sealing, skirting, cushioning, washers and bushings.

Oil Resistant Weather Resistant Low Temp Flex
NBR

Nitrile Rubber

For applications requiring high resistance to petroleum based fluids. White FDA Nitrile available for food, pharmaceutical, and cosmetic processing — non-marking, non-allergenic, safe for repeated food exposure.

Petroleum Resistant FDA Available Abrasion Resistant
EPDM

EPDM Rubber

For applications requiring higher temperature ranges, ozone, chemical and weather resistance. Excellent for outdoor applications and water/steam service.

High Temp Ozone Resistant Weather Resistant
FKM

Viton® Rubber

Premium grade fluoro-elastomer sheet for the most demanding applications. Excellent heat and chemical resistance. Not suitable for flex fuels containing high levels of alcohol or MTBE.

Chemical Resistant High Heat Premium Grade
SIL

Silicone Rubber

Designed for extreme high and low temperature resistance. Provides excellent UV and ozone resistance, is non-toxic, chemically inert and fungus resistant.

Extreme Temps UV Resistant Non-Toxic
GUM

Tan Gum Rubber

Excellent physical properties — superior resilience, tensile, elongation, and abrasion resistance. FDA approved ingredients, non-marking, safe for repeated food exposure. Good tear strength, resistant to water and mild acids.

FDA Approved Non-Marking Food Safe
